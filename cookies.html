Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version

This website doesn't use cookies. It also doesn't use local storage, session storage, tracking pixels, analytics or fingerprinting. That's why you don't see a cookie banner: there's nothing to consent to.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can store on your device to remember information about you, such as your preferences or your activity. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and device fingerprinting.

2. What this website uses

TechnologyUsed on lmk-marketing.vercel.app?
Cookies (first-party or third-party)No
Local storage or session storageNo
Analytics (such as Google Analytics or Vercel Analytics)No
Advertising or social-media tracking pixelsNo
Embedded third-party players or widgetsNo. Our social-media buttons are plain links.
Web fonts from Google FontsYes. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies, but Google receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load. See our Privacy Policy.

3. When other websites may use cookies

Other services may set their own cookies, under their own policies, when you:

  • click through to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or WhatsApp;
  • send the contact form, which passes briefly through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) before returning you to our website.

4. Do we need your consent?

Consent is needed for cookies and trackers that aren't strictly necessary. This website uses none, so we don't show a consent banner. If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential cookie, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent before setting it.

5. Controlling cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. This affects other websites you visit, not this one, because this site sets none.
