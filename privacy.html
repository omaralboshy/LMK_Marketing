4. What we don't do

  • No cookies, local storage, analytics, heat-maps, advertising pixels or fingerprinting on this website. See our Cookie Policy.
  • No embedded social-media players or widgets that track you. Our Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and WhatsApp buttons are plain links, so nothing from those platforms loads unless you click them.
  • No selling, renting or trading of personal data.
  • No automated decision-making or profiling.
  • No marketing emails or newsletters unless you explicitly ask for them.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside the United Arab Emirates, for example in the United States or the European Union. When personal data leaves the UAE, we rely on the cross-border transfer provisions of the PDPL. Depending on the case, that means the destination offers adequate protection, the transfer is needed to answer your request or perform our agreement with you, or you have consented.

7. How we protect your data

  • The website is served only over encrypted HTTPS, with strict browser security policies that block untrusted scripts, framing and injected content.
  • The website itself stores no personal data. It has no database, no user accounts and no tracking, so there is nothing on the site for an attacker to steal.
  • Messages are kept in accounts that only authorised LMK team members can access, protected by strong passwords and two-step verification.
  • If a personal-data breach happens, we will notify the UAE Data Office and the affected people where the PDPL requires it.

8. Your rights

Subject to the conditions of the PDPL, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or stop certain processing;
  • transfer your data to another organisation where that's technically possible;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This doesn't affect anything we lawfully did before you withdrew it.

To make a request, email lmkwhat2026@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can also complain to the UAE Data Office.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. This website isn't directed at anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a minor has sent us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Other websites

Links to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp and FormSubmit take you to websites that we don't control. Their privacy policies apply once you're there.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the way we handle personal data changes, for example if we ever add analytics. The date at the top shows the latest version.

12. Contact

For any privacy question, email lmkwhat2026@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request".
